- Paladin — Oath of the Badge (weird west)
- Rogue — Detective (noir)
- Rogue — Gambler (weird west)
- Wizard — School of Spellslinging (weird west)

Artificer, Barbarian, Cleric, Fighter, Ranger, Sorcerer, and Warlock were in the class list with no subclasses.
